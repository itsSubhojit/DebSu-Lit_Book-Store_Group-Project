Customer Satisfaction

Refund & Return Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026 · Fair Reader Guarantee

Summary: We stand behind the quality of every book we ship. If a book arrives damaged, defective, or misprinted, we provide full replacements or refunds within 7 days of delivery.

1. Eligible Return Situations

You may initiate a return or replacement if:

  • The book arrived physically damaged (torn pages, broken binding, water damage).
  • The book has manufacturing defects (missing pages, inverted printing, misbound text).
  • You received an incorrect title or edition differing from your order confirmation.

2. Non-Returnable Items

Returns are not accepted for:

  • Books showing signs of customer usage, markings, or folded pages after delivery.
  • Digital downloads or promotional gift vouchers.
  • Requests submitted after the 7-day return window.

3. Return Procedure

To initiate a return:

  1. Take clear photos of the book, packaging, and parcel shipping label.
  2. Email your order ID and photos to returns@debsulit.com within 7 days of receipt.
  3. Our support team will verify the claim within 48 hours and dispatch a free replacement or approve a return pickup.

4. Refund Processing & Timelines

Once a return is received and inspected at our fulfillment center:

  • Refunds are processed back to the original payment method used during checkout via Razorpay.
  • Refunds typically reflect in your bank account, card, or UPI within 5 to 7 business days depending on your issuing bank.
