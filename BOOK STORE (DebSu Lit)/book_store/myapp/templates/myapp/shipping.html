Delivery & Fulfillment

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026 · Nationwide Dispatch Across India

Summary: We carefully pack and dispatch orders within 24 to 48 hours. Orders across major Indian metros are delivered in 3 to 5 business days, with real-time tracking provided upon dispatch.

1. Dispatch Timelines

Orders placed Monday through Saturday before 3:00 PM IST are processed and packed within 24 to 48 hours. Orders placed on Sundays or national holidays are dispatched on the subsequent business day.

2. Estimated Delivery Transit Times

  • Metro Cities (Kolkata, Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad): 2 to 4 business days.
  • Tier-2 & Tier-3 Cities: 4 to 6 business days.
  • North-East, J&K, and Remote Locations: 6 to 9 business days.

3. Shipping Rates

We believe in transparent pricing:

  • Standard Orders: Free standard shipping on all orders above ₹499.
  • Sub-Threshold Orders: A flat delivery fee of ₹49 is applied at checkout.

4. Parcel Packaging & Courier Partners

All books are cushioned with reinforced cardboard mailers to prevent corner dents and weather damage in transit. We partner with reliable Indian logistics carriers including Blue Dart, Delhivery, and India Post Speed Post.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order has shipped, you will receive an SMS and email notification containing your airway bill (AWB) number and direct parcel tracking link.

Tracking Inquiries

If your package is delayed past the estimated window, contact fulfillment at:

Email: shipping@debsulit.com
Track Online: Visit your My Orders page.
