Pricing & Billing Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026 · Fair & Honest Bookshop Pricing

Summary: All prices displayed on DebSu Lit are in Indian Rupees (INR, ₹), include applicable taxes (GST where mandated), and reflect genuine publisher Recommended Retail Prices (MRP) with transparent discounts.

1. Transparent Currency & Pricing

All items on our platform are priced and settled exclusively in Indian Rupees (INR). When you browse titles, the price displayed on the book detail card and in your cart is the exact base price payable, without hidden platform surcharges.

2. Discounts & Publisher MRP

We source authentic editions directly from authorized publishers and literary distributors. Promotional sale prices and discount tags compare against the publisher's stated Maximum Retail Price (MRP).

3. Taxes & Invoicing

In accordance with Indian Goods & Services Tax (GST) laws, printed books currently enjoy an exemption under GST tariff classifications. Where applicable for companion stationery or packaging, taxes are calculated and explicitly broken down on your digital tax invoice sent after order confirmation.

4. Price Changes & Accuracy

Publishers periodically revise print edition prices between reprint runs. We strive to maintain absolute catalog accuracy; in the rare case an item is listed with an erroneous price, our team will notify you prior to dispatch to reconfirm or provide a full immediate refund.

5. Accepted Payment Methods

Through our secure Razorpay integration, we support:

  • Unified Payments Interface (UPI): Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and bank UPI apps.
  • Cards: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, and Maestro debit & credit cards issued in India.
  • Netbanking: 50+ major Indian scheduled commercial banks.
  • Wallets: Leading digital wallets supported by Razorpay.
